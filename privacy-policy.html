Privacy Policy — Happy Trucker AI Navigator

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Developer: Happy Trucker (Sam Wi)
Contact: wiss2234@gmail.com

This privacy policy explains what data the Happy Trucker AI Navigator Android app collects, how it is used, and what is shared with third parties.

1. Data the app collects

Precise location (GPS). While you use navigation and driving-analysis features, the app reads your device's location, speed, heading, and altitude. This data is stored on your device and is used only to provide navigation, safe-speed guidance, and driving analysis.

Trip information. Information you enter or the app records during a trip — such as truck/trailer type, cargo weight, origin and destination, and driving/stop times — is stored on your device only.

AI assistant queries. When you ask the in-app AI assistant (Sofia) a question, the question text and relevant driving context (for example, your current location when you ask about weather ahead) are sent through our relay server to the OpenAI API so an answer can be generated. The app has no accounts: we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account identifier, and none is sent with these requests.

Weather and road data. Your approximate location is sent to our weather-data provider to fetch forecasts and road-condition information for your route.

Maps. Map display and routing use Google Maps Platform, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How we use the data

Location and trip data are used solely to operate the app's features: navigation, safe-speed recommendations, weather and road-condition analysis, crosswind alerts, and AI driving assistance. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to operate the features described above (map provider, weather-data provider, and OpenAI via our relay server), and only the data each feature requires.

4. Data retention and deletion

Location and trip data remain on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all on-device data. AI queries are processed by OpenAI under OpenAI's API platform policies.

5. Security

Data sent over the network is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The OpenAI API key is kept on our relay server and is never embedded in the app.

6. Your choices

You may deny location permission in your device settings, but navigation and driving-analysis features will not work without it. You may delete all on-device data at any time by uninstalling the app.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
